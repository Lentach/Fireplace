User Card / Profile — Round 1 directions

All three directions share: no per-card Chat Background control (moves to Settings → Appearance), live data (changes apply in place, no nav bounce), working 3-photo gallery with segment indicator, and a Telegram-style collapse — scrolling down shrinks the photo into the small circle avatar in the top bar. Differences are the identity hero and the avatar-management model.

Direction 1 — “Telegram Full-Bleed”

Square full-bleed hero (shorter than today: 300 logical px, not 390), scrim only at the edges, segment strip top-right, name+status pinned to the photo. Self profile: the gallery IS the manager — the ✎ button on the photo opens a glass sheet with actions for the viewed photo. Collapse: photo shrinks to the 38px circle in the bar (Hero-style), title fades in.
