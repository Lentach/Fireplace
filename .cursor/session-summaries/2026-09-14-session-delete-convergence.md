- **`4e38c417` — REVERTED my own tombstone design and fixed the real remainder.** I proposed and began building server-side tombstones (migration 0018, entity, per-user `clearedAt`, serve field, retention cron) for offline convergence. Wrong twice: (1) `getServedMessageIds` → `reconcileStoredPlaintext` already does this authoritatively and is a documented invariant with four red-tested properties; (2) a durable ledger of what was deleted and when is a privacy liability inside a privacy fix.
