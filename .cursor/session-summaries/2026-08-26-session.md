# App icon replaced: silver hex (Umbra concept), full asset regeneration

**Date:** 2026-08-26

## What was done

- **Rebrand exploration (naming + icon).** Owner wants to move away from "Fireplace" (app is hex-themed now). His "Hexapod" idea was argued down (a *bug* is the worst association for an E2E messenger). Candidate names explored across two rounds; owner liked **Umbra** (the total shadow of an eclipse — "inside the umbra, no one sees in"). Collision check: no messenger named Umbra (only a home-network-security iOS app + the furniture brand, different classes). **Name is NOT final and nothing name-related was changed** — app is still "Fireplace" everywhere.
- **Icon design iterations** (all masters in gitignored `.planning/branding/`, preview pages `preview*.html` there too): scenes (starfield/eclipse/backlit sun) → rejected after Apple HIG research (one element, one focal point, simple background); iOS 18 tinted-mode was simulated via luminance mapping and inverted an advisory's guess — thick pale outline survives grayscale, dark solid fill vanishes. Owner picked the **tinted-S3 silver look, bare hex, no glint**: thin silver-gradient hex outline on near-black (`umbra-s5-silver.svg`, 722 B master).
- **Small-size fix after measurement:** at 48px the bottom rim nearly dropped out (luma 27 vs bg 7 at the bottom vertex). Bottom gradient stop `#333333`→`#4A4A4A`, stroke 10→12 → vertex luma 49, edges 73–97. Applied identically to the adaptive-foreground master. Before/after shown to owner (`preview-v7.html`); approved ("mmm perfection").
- **Full asset generation** via `flutter_launcher_icons` v0.14.4 (config at `frontend/flutter_launcher_icons.yaml`): Android mipmaps + adaptive (foreground = hex at 58% on transparency, survives the ~66% crop), full iOS appiconset, web icons + maskable + favicon. `remove_alpha_ios: true` added (App Store rejects alpha). Notification icons hand-rasterized: `notification-icon-512/192` from the same master, `notification-badge-96` pure white-on-transparent (the alpha-mask contract in `web-push-sw.js:205-208`).
- **Regression guarded:** the generator flipped `manifest.json` background/theme `#F7F4F0`→`#000000`; reverted and set the yaml `web:` colors to cream so future runs are idempotent (proven by re-run → zero manifest diff).
- **SVG→PNG pipeline established on this box** (no inkscape/magick/cairosvg): `@resvg/resvg-js` via real node in `.planning/branding/rasterize/` (`rasterize.mjs <svg> <png> <size>`).
